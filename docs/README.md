# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This folder contains OctoAcme’s project management process documentation. At a high level, OctoAcme follows a structured lifecycle: **initiation** (define problem, goals, stakeholders, and go/no-go), **planning** (build prioritized backlog, milestones, dependencies, and Definition of Done), **execution and tracking** (deliver in increments with regular demos and metrics), **release and deployment** (pre-release quality gates, rollout, and verification), and **retrospective/continuous improvement** (capture learnings and track action items).

Delivery is supported by clear cross-functional ownership. **Project Managers** coordinate schedules, risks, and communication; **Product Managers/Leads** define outcomes and prioritize value; **Developers** implement and test solutions; **QA/testing** validates acceptance criteria and release readiness; and **stakeholders/sponsors** provide direction and decision support at key points.

Communication is managed through regular standups, weekly syncs, milestone demos, and stakeholder updates, with a defined escalation path from team triage to PM, Product Lead, and Sponsor. Quality is integrated throughout delivery via acceptance criteria, CI checks (tests/lint/security scanning), targeted manual QA when needed, and pre-release verification with rollback readiness.
