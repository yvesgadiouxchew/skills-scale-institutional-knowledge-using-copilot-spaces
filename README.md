## Project management process docs

This repository includes a lightweight OctoAcme project management playbook for guiding work from intake through delivery and continuous improvement.

### Process summary

- **Overview:** shared principles, core roles, artifacts, lifecycle, and communication cadence.
- **Initiation:** confirm the business need, identify stakeholders, define success metrics, and decide whether to move into planning.
- **Planning:** turn the initiative into an actionable backlog, timeline, release plan, and risk-managed delivery approach.
- **Execution and tracking:** run day-to-day delivery through project boards, pull requests, testing, demos, and escalation paths.
- **Release and deployment:** validate release readiness, deploy safely, verify outcomes, and communicate the release.
- **Risk management and communication:** maintain a risk register and provide clear stakeholder updates and escalation paths.
- **Retrospective and continuous improvement:** capture lessons learned and convert them into concrete follow-up actions.
- **Roles and personas:** clarify responsibilities for developers, product managers, and project managers across the process.
